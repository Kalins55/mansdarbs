# Mansdarbs Voice Finance Device

## Product direction

Mansdarbs may eventually offer a purpose-built, ultra-low-cost voice-first device for everyday financial and work administration. The device is not intended to be a general smartphone. Its promise is:

**Tell Mansdarbs what happened or what needs to be done, then get on with your life.**

A user should be able to speak naturally to record income and expenses, create reminders, ask for a financial overview, prepare an invoice, or capture a work task without navigating menus or typing.

## Core interaction

1. The user presses and holds one physical microphone/talk button.
2. The device captures the user's request only while activated.
3. Speech is transcribed and interpreted by Mansdarbs.
4. Mansdarbs repeats the understood action in a short confirmation.
5. The action is saved or carried out. Financially consequential or irreversible actions require explicit confirmation.
6. The device can speak a concise response and deliver reminders.

Latvian language support is a first-release requirement for any real product. Further languages should follow the platform's regional expansion.

## Hardware ambition: €2 target

The founder's stated ambition is to explore whether a dedicated Mansdarbs device could be produced with an **aspirational target budget of €2 per unit** if a suitable phone manufacturer, OEM/ODM partner, or production opportunity becomes available.

This is a target to investigate, not a confirmed achievable retail price or bill of materials. Feasibility must be tested honestly. A complete standalone 4G cellular handset at €2 may be unrealistic once the modem, battery, display or indicators, speaker, microphone, buttons, assembly, certification, logistics, taxes, warranty, and connectivity are included.

When a manufacturing relationship or access to a phone factory becomes available, proactively reopen this concept and request a costed feasibility proposal. Compare at least:
- a minimal standalone cellular handset;
- a very low-cost voice terminal that relies on a paired phone or nearby connectivity;
- a dedicated one-button accessory that connects to a user's existing device.

Keep the €2 ambition visible in negotiations and design-to-cost work, but never misrepresent an estimate as a supplier quote or a verified production cost.

## Non-negotiable principles

- No always-on microphone. Audio capture starts only after deliberate physical activation.
- No account should be required for basic local use where technically and legally possible.
- Do not retain raw audio by default; explain any necessary transmission to speech-recognition services and minimize retention.
- Keep user financial records private and local by default wherever practical, consistent with the Mansdarbs privacy architecture.
- Repeat back important interpreted details (amount, date, recipient, category) so errors can be caught.
- Never initiate payments, submit tax declarations, or perform other consequential actions without the required explicit user confirmation.
- Make the device extremely simple, durable, repairable, and inexpensive to manufacture.

## Development sequence

1. Validate Latvian speech recognition and the voice workflow on existing phones.
2. Prototype a one-button accessory before committing to custom hardware.
3. Obtain real OEM/ODM quotations and compare component, certification, connectivity, support, and lifecycle costs.
4. Only proceed to a dedicated handset when user demand, reliability, privacy, and unit economics are demonstrated.

This document records a strategic product idea and manufacturing ambition. It does not mean hardware has been designed, ordered, certified, or built.
